Stop 1 · First

Camp Fire Long Beach

Neighborhood warm-up · free entry · ~5–10 min from home

Where
7070 E Carson St, Long Beach 90808
When
Aim Sat Oct 3 or Sun Oct 4 · noon–2 (opens noon). Third-party lists weekends Oct 3–4, 10–11, 17–18 · confirm by phone.
Cost
Entry & parking free. Pumpkins ~$10–40. Activity tickets $1 each (or 50/$45).
Kid-fit
Straw maze, bounce house, decorating, glitter tattoos. Not scary. Stroller/wagon trails (decomposed granite). Closed-toe shoes.
Tip: Call (562) 421-2725 to confirm which weekends are open before locking the date. Small lot — overflow at McBride HS.

Stop 2 · Midweek farm

Hana Field (Tanaka · Costa Mesa)

Real vines + animals · optional dinos · ~25–40 min

Where
427 Anton Blvd, Costa Mesa 92626
When
Best Wed Oct 7 or Thu Oct 8 at 9am. Season Oct 3–Nov 1 · Wed–Sun 9am–6pm (last entry 5pm).
Cost
Ages 2 & under free. Wed–Thu $23 · Fri–Sun $27. Promo through Oct 10: $23 every day + free parking. After that Fri–Sun parking $12. Cashless. Tickets required.
Kid-fit
Pumpkins off the vine, flower u-pick, barn animals. Jurassic Fright maze is daytime/family but dinos can startle — skip maze if needed.
Tip: Weekday open is the chillest window. Avoid Fri–Sun peaks and Oct 10 dog day if crowds bother the girls.

Stop 3 · Opening weekend

Cal Poly Pomona PumpkinFest

University festival finale · ~40–60 min · sells out often

Where
Kellogg Ranch · 4102 S University Dr, Pomona 91768
When
Opening weekend Sat/Sun Oct 10–11 · arrive 10am. Festival Sat/Sun only Oct 10–31 · 10am–7pm. Theme that weekend: Pumpkinzania.
Cost
Under 2 free. Press (Sep 2026) cites ~$18 online / $22 gate + fee — confirm on TicketSpice. Parking ~$5 Sat/Sun (buy with tickets).
Kid-fit
Pumpkin Hill, Jr. Farmers zone, cow train, petting farm, hay wagon. Working farm — closed-toe shoes. Use mini maze for Sloane.
Tip: Grab tickets early for opening weekend. Quiet alt: weekday Farm Store only (10am–6pm) for pumpkin shopping without the festival.

Stop 4 · Big farm day

Underwood Family Farms · Moorpark

Full harvest festival · ~1.5–2 hrs · worth the drive once

Where
3370 Sunset Valley Rd, Moorpark 93021
When
Aim Mon–Thu mid/late Oct · arrive ~1–2pm. Season Sep 26–Oct 31 · 9am–6pm.
Cost
Under 2 free · parking free. Weekdays $16 at gate only. Weekends need timed online tickets (~$25–$38 by theme).
Kid-fit
Wagon rides, corn maze, giant patch, animal center, play gear. Personal strollers/wagons welcome. Not a haunted house; Halloween Day / giant spiders may be skippable for a sensitive 2yo.
Tip: Weekday afternoons are quieter (mornings = school tours; Fridays busiest weekday). Avoid late-Oct themed weekends if possible.
